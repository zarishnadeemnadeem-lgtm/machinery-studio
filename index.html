How can I ask for a price? +

Click any “Ask for details” button or the WhatsApp button and tell us which equipment you are interested in. Prices should be confirmed directly.

Can I enquire about a specific machine? +

Yes. Share the make, model, capacity, intended use and any other specifications you know.

Do you list live stock availability? +

No live inventory is connected to this demo website. Availability and product details must be confirmed with your team.

Can I ask about spare parts or maintenance? +

Yes. Include the equipment make/model and describe the part or issue in your message.
